Brazilian Portuguese style for electionsarchive.com

Write as a careful editor at a quality Brazilian newsroom (g1's apuração pages, Folha, Estadão) and a good Brazilian product team would: precise, plain, short, and consistent. Use Brazilian spelling and usage, never European Portuguese.

Register
- Address the reader as "você", with third-person imperatives in sentences: "Escolha um partido."
- Buttons, menu items, and links use the infinitive: "Abrir mapa", "Mostrar mais", "Salvar".
- Labels, column headers, tabs, legends, and headings are noun phrases: "Comparecimento", "Votos válidos", "Distribuição de cadeiras".
- Failures: "Não foi possível carregar o mapa". Progress: "Carregando…". Use "excluir", "redefinir", "verificar", never "deletar", "resetar", "checar".
- The site never speaks as "nós": no "nós", "nosso", "nossa", "a gente". Rephrase impersonally ("O arquivo reúne ...", "... foi adicionado").
- No exclamation marks or marketing tone.

Sentences
- Less is more. Keep sentences short and in plain order. Never hang a remark on the end of a sentence after a comma (", o que ...", ", ou seja, ...", ", tornando ...", or a trailing figure or judgment); give it its own sentence or drop it. When the English is wordy, say the point more simply.
- Verbs over noun chains ("a realização do cálculo da distribuição ..."). Split long English sentences.
- Brazilian election vocabulary: "apuração", "urnas apuradas", "segundo turno", "votos válidos", "abstenção", "cadeira", "disputa".
- No "estar sendo" chains; the gerund only where Brazilians naturally use it.
- English "Today" on a label is "Hoje".

Placeholders
- Make articles and prepositions around a placeholder agree with it, or avoid them when its gender is unknown ("de {place}", not "do {place}").
- {countText}, {share}, {percent}, and {margin} arrive already formatted ("1.234", "54,2%"). Never add digits or a percent sign.

Typography
- Quotation marks “ ”. No dashes as sentence punctuation where the English has none; an en dash stays in ranges.

Follow the glossary for every electoral term.
